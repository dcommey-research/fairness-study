# Revision protocol (fixed before examining new benchmark outcomes)

This is a new experiment, not a verified reconstruction of the submitted 1,380 evaluations. The copied eight-dataset artifact has 19 configurations per dataset and reports 760 trainings; no fold records or originating experiment commit are available. Hashes are in provenance/copied_artifact_inventory.json. The author-identified submitted source was retrieved from Overleaf and preserved in provenance/submitted_overleaf; historical execution provenance and portal attachments still require verification.

## Design

Seven datasets: Adult, COMPAS, German Credit, LSAC (raw SAS source, Black/White subset, gender retained as a feature), Bank Marketing, Cleveland Heart Disease, and one canonical ACSIncome task (California 2018). Credit Approval is excluded because the authoritative UCI source anonymizes attribute semantics; the legacy A9=gender claim is unsupported. One ACS task avoids treating five survey-derived tasks as independent domains. Adult and ACS are explicitly a shared census-income source family. Domain summaries weight datasets equally within domains and domains equally, rather than pool sample counts. This convenience benchmark does not represent whole domains.

Five independently seeded repetitions of five stratified outer folds, seeds 42,123,456,789,1011. Repetition and fold are persisted. Overlapping training sets and reusing individuals across repetitions preclude treating 25 folds as independent observations. All variability and paired deltas are descriptive; no inferential fold tests or mean±1.96 SE confidence intervals. Fold SD is labeled SD. A within-test-fold, within-sensitive-group bootstrap (500 draws from confusion-cell multinomials) estimates conditional sampling variability of realized decision disparities. It fixes fitted predictions and group sizes and excludes training uncertainty. No claim of population/generalization confidence.

LR (liblinear C=1, L2, max_iter=2000), DT (depth6, min_samples_leaf=5), RF (100 trees, depth6, n_jobs=2), HGB (100 iterations, max_leaf_nodes31, learning_rate=.1, L2=0, early_stopping=False). Paired reweighing uses the same learner and preprocessing; weights P(A)P(Y)/P(A,Y), normalized to mean1. TO for each learner fits the underlying learner on 80% of the outer training data and calibrates on the remaining20%; a subset baseline uses that exact learner and preprocessing fitted only on its own training subset. Calibration labels and test rows never train preprocessing. TO uses intersectional groups, DP, accuracy objective, grid1000, predict_proba, prefit=True, flip=False. No attribute fallback; infeasibility is recorded. EG uses LR with identical hyperparameters; DP group-to-overall difference bounds .01,.05,.10; eps=.01 controls optimization, max_iter50, nu=None, eta0=2, run_linprog_step=True. It is a reduction using classification-error oracles, not adversarial training or a convexity guarantee for these implementations.

The main comparison uses realized decision accuracy and DP, with expected decision accuracy and DP of positive-decision probabilities as the primary Pareto coordinates, integrating prediction randomization. Ordinary models use deterministic 0.5-threshold decisions; EG uses its public mixture weights and component binary predictions; TO uses its pinned Fairlearn interpolated policy PMF. Seeds are persisted; 20 independent prediction draws quantify conditional randomization variability. Conventional score AUROC (predict_proba) and randomized policy AUROC (probability of a positive decision) are reported in separate columns. Neither binary decisions nor the underlying TO classifier scores substitute for mitigated risk scores. AUROC gaps across these distinct score types are not used as mitigation-performance claims. Balanced accuracy is the consistent decision-discrimination metric for every configuration; binary AUROC would duplicate it.

All evaluation groups are intersections, with census/source labels preserved. Group rows include total/positive/negative counts, selected counts, TP/FP/FN/TN, selection/TPR/FPR and Wilson widths. Missing groups are explicitly represented. DP covers observed groups; EO excludes groups with no positives; equalized odds uses groups with both labels. Each aggregate carries contributing folds and full-group-coverage counts. DI=min/max is in [0,1], undefined if max=0 or fewer than two observed groups. No fairness threshold or legal compliance claim. Positive/favorable outcomes are defined in revision/data.py, including adverse positives for COMPAS and Heart Disease. Bank subscription has no established favorable interpretation.

## Focused diagnostic

German Credit: raw integer age, age≥25, age≥35, and bands <25,25–39,≥40. Identical row indices, raw features with age excluded, targets, outer folds, inner splits, seeds, and LR baseline/RW/EG/TO configurations isolate effects of grouping on mitigation and measurement. Raw age is a finer evaluation target, not intrinsically improper coding. Report operational failures, undefined group metrics, sampling variability and post-mitigation residual DP separately. Cross-evaluate each fitted age-policy on both its native groups and a fixed age≥25 reference group; unchanged baseline predictions across encodings provide a control. A planned German subsampling sensitivity uses 250,500,1000 rows with matching subset membership across encodings and same seeds; results cannot identify general causal sample-size effects in other domains.

A practical support diagnostic flags group selection denominators <97 and group positive/negative denominators <97: a worst-case normal-approximation binomial 95% half-width exceeds .10 at n<97 (1.96/(2√n)); Wilson widths are also reported for actual rates. This is a precision budget, not a validated predictor of mitigation failure, fairness threshold, or simultaneous confidence bound. Compare conditional bootstrap spread by support flags and age encoding; report associations descriptively and hold causal claims to the controlled ablation.

## Provenance and completion

Every planned evaluation has a persisted success/failure record, warnings, hyperparameters, score kind, split identifier, predictions, policy probabilities, mitigation/evaluation groups, preprocessing fit row hashes, optimizer diagnostics, code hash and environment lock. Resume only identical protocol/code hashes. Tables, figures, and manuscript numbers must be generated from new fold records. Never claim that a count of successful dataset summaries proves every evaluation completed. New evaluation counts differ from model fits: TO reuses a subset baseline, while EG performs multiple oracle fits.
